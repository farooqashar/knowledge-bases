# Glossary

MIT has its own vocabulary. These are the terms you'll hear most.

- **Course [number]:** a department or major, as in "I'm Course 6." See [Course numbers](course-numbers.md).
- **8.01, 18.06, ...:** class numbers, said digit by digit ("eight oh one").
- **The 'Tute:** the Institute, i.e., MIT.
- **Pset:** problem set, the weekly homework.
- **GIRs:** General Institute Requirements, the classes every undergraduate takes. See [Academics](academics.md).
- **HASS:** Humanities, Arts and Social Sciences classes.
- **CI-H / CI-M:** communication-intensive classes in HASS / in the major.
- **UROP:** Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program; also used as a verb ("I'm UROPing in a robotics lab").
- **IAP:** Independent Activities Period, the January term.
- **P/NR:** pass/no record, the grading for first-semester classes.
- **Tool:** someone who studies relentlessly; also a verb ("tooling for the final").
- **Punt:** to skip or give up on something, often sleep or a pset.
- **Hosed:** overwhelmed or out of luck ("I'm totally hosed this week").
- **Drinking from a fire hose:** the experience of MIT's workload, often credited to former president Jerome Wiesner.
- **IHTFP:** officially ambiguous. To students, "I Hate This F— Place"; to the optimistic, "I've Truly Found Paradise," among many other readings.
- **Hack:** a clever, harmless prank. See [Hacks](hacks.md).
- **Brass Rat:** the class ring. See [Traditions](traditions.md).
- **Smoot:** a unit of length (5 feet 7 inches) marked along the Harvard Bridge.
- **Bruno:** an informal unit of volume, from Baker House's piano drop.
- **The Infinite:** the Infinite Corridor.
- **10-250:** the large lecture hall under the Great Dome; room numbers read building-room.
- **Lobby 7:** the main entrance on Massachusetts Avenue.
- **FSILGs:** Fraternities, Sororities and Independent Living Groups.
- **CPW:** Campus Preview Weekend for admitted students.
- **Mens et Manus:** "mind and hand," MIT's motto.
- **Tim:** Tim the Beaver, the mascot ("MIT" backwards).
- **OCW:** OpenCourseWare, MIT's free online class materials.
- **Sloanie:** a student at the Sloan School of Management.
