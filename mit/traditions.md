# Traditions

## The Brass Rat

MIT's class ring. Each class designs its own ring during sophomore year, unveils it at the fall **Ring Premiere**, and receives it at a formal **Ring Delivery** in the spring.

- **What's on it:** the beaver on the bezel, the MIT seal and the class year on the shanks, the Boston and Cambridge skylines on the sides, and a map of campus inside.
- **The name:** the beaver looks a bit like a rat, and "gold beaver" became "Brass Rat," even though the ring is gold, platinum or stainless steel.
- **How it's worn:** while you're a student, the beaver faces you, and so does the Boston skyline, the world waiting outside. At graduation you turn the ring around, so the beaver faces out and the Cambridge skyline faces you, as a reminder of MIT.
- Graduate students have their own version, the **Grad Rat**.

## Tim the Beaver

MIT's mascot. The beaver was chosen in 1914 as "nature's engineer." The name "Tim" is "MIT" spelled backwards.

## Mens et Manus

MIT's motto, Latin for "mind and hand": learning by doing, theory joined to practice.

## The smoot

In October 1958, Lambda Chi Alpha pledges measured the Harvard Bridge using pledge Oliver Smoot, who was 5 feet 7 inches tall, as the ruler. The bridge came to **364.4 smoots, plus or minus one ear**. The markings are repainted to this day. Smoot later chaired the American National Standards Institute and led the International Organization for Standardization.

## Pi Day admissions

MIT releases regular admissions decisions on **Pi Day, March 14**, at **6:28 p.m.**, "tau time" (τ ≈ 6.28).

## IAP and Mystery Hunt

- **IAP**, the four-week January term, is filled with short, often unusual classes. It ends with **Charm School**, a half-day of lessons in etiquette and social skills.
- **Mystery Hunt** takes place over Martin Luther King Jr. Day weekend during IAP. It's one of the world's oldest puzzle hunts: teams solve hundreds of interlocking puzzles to find a coin hidden on campus, and the winning team writes the next year's hunt.

## More traditions

- **Campus Preview Weekend (CPW):** admitted students visit in April, sit in on classes and stay with current students.
- **Drop Date:** Baker House drops an old piano off its roof each spring. The dent it leaves gave rise to the "Bruno," an informal unit of volume.
- **Robot competitions:** 2.007 (Mechanical Engineering's robot contest) and student-run contests like Battlecode during IAP.
- **Steer Roast:** a Senior House tradition from 1964 to 2017, when the event was cancelled and the dorm closed soon after.
