- [Traditions](traditions.md): the Brass Rat, Tim the Beaver, the smoot, Pi Day admissions and Mystery Hunt
- [Hacks](hacks.md): what a hack is, and the most famous ones
- [Glossary](glossary.md): MIT vocabulary from "pset" to "IHTFP"
